# ABS-494 — scoring & fusion experiment matrix (k=10)

Generated by `scripts/eval_retrieval_experiment.py`. Every arm is the production `RetrievalService.search()` with `_merge_channel_scores` swapped (and, for the FTS family, one extra channel to merge) — see that script's docstring for what each family is. The `current` arm overrides nothing and runs straight through production.

**The `current` arm always tracks production.** It is not a frozen historical control: it runs the shipped `search()` with nothing overridden, so every delta here is quoted against the retriever we actually serve. This matters because this matrix has already been wrong once the other way — its first run (commit `702cb4c`) graded a control scoring 0.1618 that ABS-492 and ABS-500 had already moved to 0.5588, and recommended arms dev had overtaken. Those numbers are not comparable to these and are not carried forward.

ABS-494 shipped `fts_hybrid_50` (`docs/decisions/ABS-494-SCORING-FUSION-DECISION.md`). A run made *after* that ship should therefore show `current` and `fts_hybrid_50` agreeing exactly — that agreement is the check that the arm which was measured and the channel that is served are the same program, not a redundant row.

**Label tier:** agent-drafted, pending human spot-check · `review_status: unreviewed`. These numbers grade **ranking, never correctness**, and are never averaged with a golden pass rate (`evals/retrieval/README.md`).
